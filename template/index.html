Project Overview

Electric vehicles are becoming an important part of the transition towards cleaner and more sustainable transportation. Understanding EV market performance helps us explore sales patterns, technology choices, regional differences and customer preferences.

This project presents an analysis of electric vehicle market performance using interactive Tableau visualizations. It brings together different charts to make the available data easier to understand and compare.

The analysis covers battery types, market regions, drivetrain categories, customer ratings, sales by model year and sales by vehicle category. Each visualization highlights a different aspect of the dataset and helps users explore the information from multiple perspectives.

Data Analysis Methodology

The project uses a structured visualization approach to explore and present the available EV market information.

  1. Data Understanding: Identify the variables available in the dataset, including model year, market region, battery type, drivetrain, category, sales and customer ratings.
  2. Data Visualization: Represent the data using pie charts, a heatmap, a donut chart, a horizontal bar chart, a line chart and a bar chart.
  3. Comparison: Compare categories, regional values and year-wise sales patterns using the visual representations.
  4. Interpretation: Examine chart labels, legends, filters and tooltips to understand the displayed results.
  5. Presentation: Embed the Tableau dashboard and story into a website built with HTML and CSS.
Tools and Technologies: Tableau Public for data visualization, HTML for webpage structure and CSS for styling and layout.

Visualization Analysis

The dashboard includes six visualizations. Each chart provides a different way to examine the EV market data.

1. Battery Type Pie Chart

The pie chart shows how the data is distributed across battery types. The size of each slice helps compare the relative share of each type.

Analysis: Compare the largest and smallest slices to identify the most and least represented battery types.

2. Market Region Heatmap

The heatmap compares values across different market regions and model years. Color intensity helps highlight differences in the displayed data.

Analysis: Look for region-year combinations with comparatively high or low values.

3. Drivetrain Donut Chart

The donut chart illustrates the distribution of drivetrain categories. Its segments help viewers compare the categories represented in the dataset.

Analysis: Compare segment sizes to understand the relative distribution.

4. Customer Rating Bar Chart

The horizontal bar chart compares customer ratings across vehicle categories. The bar lengths reflect the values shown on the chart's rating scale.

Analysis: Identify categories with relatively higher and lower displayed ratings.

5. Sales by Model Year

The line chart shows changes in sales across model years. It helps reveal trends, fluctuations and changes over time.

Analysis: Examine peaks, dips and changes in the line.

6. Sales by Category

The bar chart compares sales values across vehicle categories. The relative heights of the bars make category comparisons easier.

Analysis: Compare categories with the highest and lowest displayed sales.

Scope and Limitations

The analysis depends on the dataset and the measures used in the Tableau workbook.

  • Results depend on the accuracy and completeness of the underlying dataset.
  • Visual patterns alone do not establish the reasons behind changes in sales or ratings.
  • Sales and ratings must be interpreted according to the units, aggregation and filters used.
  • Broader market conclusions require data that adequately represents the market being studied.

Conclusion

The Electric Vehicles Market Performance project demonstrates how data visualization can simplify the exploration of electric vehicle market information. The Tableau dashboard brings together six visualizations covering sales, regions, battery types, drivetrain categories and customer ratings.

The line chart supports the examination of year-wise sales trends, while the heatmap helps compare regional values. The pie and donut charts summarize technology distributions, and the bar charts provide comparisons across vehicle categories and customer ratings.

Overall, the project provides an interactive foundation for exploring EV market data. Combining the dashboard with the Tableau Story makes the analysis easier to navigate and supports further investigation of the patterns shown in the dataset.
